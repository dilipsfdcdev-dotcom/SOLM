Title: How to Troubleshoot Outlook Email Issues
Category: Software
Tags: Outlook, email, not sending, not receiving, sync, out-of-office, mail

Problem:
Outlook is not sending or receiving emails, is experiencing sync issues, or you need help configuring out-of-office replies.

Symptoms:
- Emails stuck in the Outbox and not sending
- Not receiving new emails or inbox not updating
- "Disconnected" or "Trying to connect" status in the Outlook footer
- Calendar or contacts not syncing across devices

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Check your internet connection.
- Confirm you can access websites in your browser
- If on VPN, verify the VPN connection is active and stable
- Look at the Outlook status bar at the bottom — it should say "Connected to: Microsoft Exchange"

Step 2: Restart Outlook.
- Close Outlook completely (check the system tray to ensure it is fully closed)
- Wait 10 seconds, then reopen Outlook
- Check if emails begin sending and receiving normally

Step 3: Repair your Outlook profile.
- Go to File > Account Settings > Account Settings
- Select your email account and click "Repair"
- Follow the prompts to let Outlook reconfigure your connection
- Restart Outlook after the repair completes

Step 4: Clear the Outlook cache.
- Close Outlook completely
- On Windows: navigate to %localappdata%\Microsoft\Outlook\RoamCache
- Delete all files in the RoamCache folder
- Reopen Outlook and allow it to rebuild the cache (this may take several minutes)

Step 5: Reconfigure your account if the issue persists.
- Go to File > Account Settings > Account Settings
- Remove the existing email account
- Add the account again using your corporate email address
- Outlook will auto-detect Exchange settings and reconfigure
- Contact IT Helpdesk if auto-configuration fails

Expected Resolution Time: Within 1 hour during business hours.
