Title: How to Troubleshoot Microsoft Teams Issues
Category: Software
Tags: Teams, Microsoft Teams, audio, video, screen sharing, chat, loading

Problem:
Microsoft Teams is not loading, or you are experiencing audio, video, or screen sharing problems during calls and meetings.

Symptoms:
- Teams app stuck on loading screen or showing a blank white window
- No audio or microphone not working during calls
- Camera not turning on or showing a black screen
- Screen sharing not available or recipients cannot see your shared screen

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Clear the Teams cache.
- Close Microsoft Teams completely (right-click the Teams icon in the system tray and select "Quit")
- On Windows: navigate to %appdata%\Microsoft\Teams and delete all files and folders inside
- On Mac: navigate to ~/Library/Application Support/Microsoft/Teams and delete all files and folders
- Reopen Teams and sign in again

Step 2: Update Microsoft Teams.
- Open Teams and click your profile picture in the top-right corner
- Select "Check for updates"
- Allow the update to download and install, then restart Teams
- Keeping Teams updated resolves many known issues

Step 3: Check audio and video settings.
- In Teams, click the three dots (...) next to your profile picture and select "Settings"
- Go to "Devices" and verify the correct microphone, speaker, and camera are selected
- Click "Make a test call" to verify audio is working
- Ensure your browser or OS has granted Teams permission to access the microphone and camera

Step 4: Test in a web browser.
- Open a browser and go to https://teams.microsoft.com
- Sign in with your corporate credentials
- Join a test meeting to see if the issue persists in the browser version
- If the browser version works, the desktop app may need reinstallation

Step 5: Reinstall Microsoft Teams.
- Uninstall Teams from Settings > Apps (Windows) or Applications folder (Mac)
- Download the latest version from the Self-Service Portal or https://teams.microsoft.com/downloads
- Install and sign in with your corporate credentials
- Contact IT Helpdesk if issues continue after reinstallation

Expected Resolution Time: Within 30 minutes for self-service troubleshooting.
