Title: How to Troubleshoot VPN Connection Issues
Category: Network/Connectivity
Tags: VPN, connection, disconnecting, slow VPN, remote access

Problem:
You are unable to connect to the corporate VPN, the VPN keeps disconnecting, or the VPN connection is unusually slow.

Symptoms:
- VPN client shows "Connection failed" or "Unable to reach server"
- VPN connects briefly then disconnects
- Extremely slow network speeds while connected to VPN
- "Authentication failed" error when entering VPN credentials

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Check your internet connection.
- Open a web browser and navigate to a public website (e.g., google.com)
- If the internet is not working, troubleshoot your local network first
- Try switching between WiFi and a wired connection

Step 2: Confirm you are using a company-approved laptop.
- VPN access is restricted to company-managed devices
- Personal devices may be blocked by security policies
- Check with IT if you are unsure whether your device is enrolled

Step 3: Restart the VPN client.
- Close the VPN application completely (check the system tray)
- Wait 10 seconds, then reopen the VPN client
- Attempt to connect again using your corporate credentials

Step 4: Clear the VPN cache.
- Open the VPN client settings
- Look for an option to clear cache or reset the connection profile
- On Windows: navigate to C:\ProgramData\VPNClient and delete temporary files
- On Mac: go to ~/Library/Application Support/VPNClient and remove cache files

Step 5: Check your firewall settings.
- Ensure your local firewall is not blocking the VPN application
- Temporarily disable third-party antivirus or firewall software to test
- Re-enable security software after testing

Step 6: Reinstall the VPN client.
- Uninstall the current VPN client from your computer
- Download the latest version from the company self-service portal
- Install and configure using your corporate credentials
- If the issue persists, contact IT Helpdesk with error details

Expected Resolution Time: Within 1 hour during business hours.
