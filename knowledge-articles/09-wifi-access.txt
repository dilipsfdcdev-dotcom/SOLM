Title: How to Connect to Corporate or Guest WiFi
Category: Network/Connectivity
Tags: WiFi, wireless, corporate network, guest WiFi, connection dropping, network

Problem:
You need to connect to the corporate or guest WiFi network, or your WiFi connection keeps dropping.

Symptoms:
- Cannot find the corporate WiFi network in available networks
- "Unable to connect" error when selecting the WiFi network
- WiFi connects but drops frequently or has no internet access
- Guest or visitor needs temporary WiFi access

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Select the correct network.
- Corporate employees: connect to "CORP-SECURE" (requires corporate credentials)
- Guests and visitors: connect to "CORP-GUEST" (requires a guest access code from reception)
- Do not connect to unknown or unsecured networks on company premises

Step 2: Enter your credentials.
- For CORP-SECURE: enter your corporate username (email) and password when prompted
- For CORP-GUEST: enter the temporary access code provided by your host or reception
- Accept the certificate prompt if one appears during connection

Step 3: Forget and rejoin the network if you cannot connect.
- On Windows: go to Settings > Network & Internet > WiFi > Manage known networks, find the network, and click "Forget"
- On Mac: go to System Preferences > Network > WiFi > Advanced, select the network, and click the minus button
- Reconnect to the network and enter your credentials again

Step 4: Check airplane mode and WiFi toggle.
- Ensure airplane mode is turned off
- Verify the WiFi toggle is turned on in your system settings
- On laptops, check for a physical WiFi switch or function key (e.g., Fn+F2)

Step 5: Update network drivers.
- On Windows: open Device Manager > Network adapters, right-click your WiFi adapter, and select "Update driver"
- Restart your computer after updating the driver
- If the issue persists, contact IT Helpdesk for further troubleshooting

Expected Resolution Time: Within 15 minutes for self-service; within 1 hour with IT assistance.
