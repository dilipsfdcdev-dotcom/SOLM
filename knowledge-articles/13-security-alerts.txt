Title: How to Handle Phishing Emails and Security Alerts
Category: Security
Tags: phishing, security, suspicious email, malware, security warning, compromised account

Problem:
You received a suspicious or phishing email, noticed unusual activity on your account, or received a security warning on your device.

Symptoms:
- Email from an unknown sender asking you to click a link or open an attachment
- Email that appears to be from a colleague but has an unusual request or tone
- Security warning about unauthorized login attempts on your account
- Pop-up alerts warning about malware or virus infections

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Do not click any links or open attachments.
- If the email looks suspicious, do not click any links, download attachments, or reply
- Do not forward the email to others except the security team
- If you already clicked a link, disconnect from the network immediately and proceed to Step 3

Step 2: Report the phishing email.
- In Outlook, select the suspicious email and click the "Report Phishing" button in the toolbar
- If the button is not available, forward the email as an attachment to security@company.com
- Include a brief note describing why you believe the email is suspicious
- Delete the email from your inbox after reporting

Step 3: Change your password if you suspect your account is compromised.
- If you clicked a suspicious link or entered credentials on a fake website, change your password immediately
- Go to the company identity portal and reset your password
- Also reset the password on any other accounts where you use the same credentials
- Enable MFA if it is not already active on your account

Step 4: Contact the security team.
- Call the IT Security Hotline or submit a security incident via the IT Helpdesk portal
- Provide details: what you clicked, what information you entered, and when it happened
- The security team will investigate and take protective actions on your account
- Follow any additional instructions provided by the security team

Expected Resolution Time: Immediate response from the security team during business hours; full investigation within 24 hours.
