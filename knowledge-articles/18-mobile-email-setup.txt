Title: How to Set Up Corporate Email on a Mobile Device
Category: Software
Tags: mobile, email, Exchange, phone, MDM, mobile device management, sync, iOS, Android

Problem:
You need to set up corporate email on your personal or company-issued mobile device to access email, calendar, and contacts on the go.

Symptoms:
- No corporate email configured on your phone or tablet
- Need to access work email and calendar from a mobile device
- New phone and need to reconfigure corporate email
- Email was working on the mobile device but stopped syncing

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Open the mail app on your device.
- On iPhone/iPad: open the Settings app and navigate to Mail > Accounts > Add Account
- On Android: open the Gmail app or the built-in Email app and go to Settings > Add Account
- Alternatively, download and install the Microsoft Outlook app from the App Store or Google Play (recommended)

Step 2: Add an Exchange account.
- Select "Microsoft Exchange" or "Exchange ActiveSync" as the account type
- If using the Outlook app, select "Add Account" and enter your corporate email address
- The app may automatically detect your organization's settings

Step 3: Enter your email and server details.
- Enter your full corporate email address (e.g., firstname.lastname@company.com)
- Enter your corporate password
- If prompted for server details, use: mail.company.com
- Set the domain to your corporate domain if required (e.g., CORP)

Step 4: Accept the MDM (Mobile Device Management) policy.
- You will be prompted to accept the company's MDM policy on your device
- This policy may enforce a screen lock, remote wipe capability, and encryption
- Review the policy details and tap "Accept" or "Agree"
- Personal devices must meet minimum security requirements before enrollment

Step 5: Verify sync is working.
- After setup, wait a few minutes for the initial sync to complete
- Verify that your inbox, calendar, and contacts are appearing on the device
- Send a test email from your phone to confirm outgoing mail works
- If sync fails, remove the account and repeat the setup process
- Contact IT Helpdesk if the issue persists after re-adding the account

Expected Resolution Time: 15 minutes for self-service setup.
