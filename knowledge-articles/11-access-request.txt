Title: How to Request Access to Systems, Shared Drives, or Applications
Category: Access/Permissions
Tags: access, permissions, shared drive, application access, system access, authorization

Problem:
You need access to a corporate system, shared drive, or application that you currently do not have permissions for.

Symptoms:
- "Access denied" or "You do not have permission" error when opening a file or application
- Unable to see a shared drive or folder that your team uses
- New role or project requires access to additional systems
- Recently joined employee missing standard access

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Identify the system or resource you need access to.
- Note the exact name of the application, shared drive, or system (e.g., Salesforce, \\fileserver\marketing, SAP)
- Determine the level of access needed (read-only, edit, admin)
- Check with your team lead or manager to confirm you need this access

Step 2: Submit an access request form.
- Log in to the IT Helpdesk portal (https://helpdesk.company.com)
- Select "Access Request" from the service catalog
- Fill in the resource name, access level, and business justification
- Specify whether the access is temporary or permanent

Step 3: Manager approval.
- Your direct manager will receive an email to approve the request
- Some systems also require approval from the resource owner or data steward
- Follow up with your manager if approval is not completed within 2 business days
- Requests without sufficient business justification may be denied

Step 4: Wait for provisioning and verify access.
- Standard access requests are provisioned within 1-3 business days after approval
- You will receive an email confirmation when access has been granted
- Log out and log back in to ensure the new permissions take effect
- Contact IT Helpdesk if access is not working after confirmation

Expected Resolution Time: 1-3 business days after manager approval.
