Title: How to Fix Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Issues
Category: Access/Permissions
Tags: MFA, authentication, codes not working, not receiving codes, 2FA, login issues

Problem:
Your MFA codes are not being accepted or you are not receiving authentication codes on your device.

Symptoms:
- "Invalid code" error when entering the MFA code
- MFA code expires before you can enter it
- Push notification not received on your phone
- Authenticator app shows no codes for your corporate account

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Check the time sync on your phone.
- MFA codes are time-based and require an accurate clock
- On iPhone: go to Settings > General > Date & Time and enable "Set Automatically"
- On Android: go to Settings > System > Date & Time and enable "Automatic date & time"
- After correcting the time, try entering a fresh code immediately

Step 2: Use a backup code.
- Locate the backup codes you saved during MFA setup
- Enter a backup code instead of the authenticator code at the login prompt
- Each backup code can only be used once
- If you have no remaining backup codes, proceed to Step 3

Step 3: Re-register your device.
- Log in to the company identity portal using a backup code or trusted device
- Navigate to Security Settings > Multi-Factor Authentication
- Remove the old device registration
- Set up MFA again by following the standard enrollment process (see article 03-mfa-setup)
- Scan the new QR code with your authenticator app

Step 4: Contact IT if you cannot resolve the issue.
- If you cannot log in at all, contact IT Helpdesk by phone or in person
- Be prepared to verify your identity with your employee ID and a photo ID
- IT can temporarily disable MFA to allow you to re-enroll
- A temporary access window of 30 minutes will be provided to complete setup

Expected Resolution Time: Within 1 hour during business hours.
