Title: How to Reset Your Password
Category: Access/Permissions
Tags: password, reset, locked out, login, credentials

Problem:
You are unable to log in because you forgot your password or your account is locked.

Symptoms:
- "Invalid password" error when logging in
- "Account locked" message after multiple failed attempts
- Forgot password and cannot access system

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Try the self-service password reset.
- Go to the login page of the application (Windows, Salesforce, SAP, VPN, etc.)
- Click "Forgot Password" or "Reset Password"
- Enter your registered email address
- Check your email (including spam/junk folder) for the reset link
- Click the link and set a new password

Step 2: If self-service reset is not available or the link expired:
- Wait 15 minutes if your account is locked — it may auto-unlock
- Try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then attempt login again

Step 3: Password requirements:
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character
- Cannot reuse your last 5 passwords

Step 4: If you still cannot reset your password:
- Contact IT Helpdesk to submit a password reset request
- You will need to verify your identity with your employee ID and registered email

Expected Resolution Time: Within 2 hours during business hours.
