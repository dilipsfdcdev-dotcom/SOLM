Title: How to Request a Replacement for a Broken or Damaged Device
Category: Hardware
Tags: device, replacement, broken, damaged, laptop, hardware, asset tag

Problem:
Your company-issued device is broken, damaged, or no longer functioning and needs to be replaced.

Symptoms:
- Laptop screen is cracked or display is malfunctioning
- Device will not power on or frequently shuts down unexpectedly
- Keyboard, trackpad, or ports are physically damaged
- Battery no longer holds a charge or device overheats constantly

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Document the issue.
- Take note of the specific problem (e.g., cracked screen, will not boot, water damage)
- Take photos of any visible physical damage
- Note when the issue started and any events that may have caused it

Step 2: Locate the asset tag on your device.
- Find the asset tag sticker on the bottom or back of your device (e.g., ASSET-00012345)
- If the sticker is missing, check your email for the original provisioning confirmation
- You can also find the asset tag in the IT Helpdesk portal under "My Devices"

Step 3: Submit a replacement request.
- Log in to the IT Helpdesk portal (https://helpdesk.company.com)
- Select "Hardware Request" > "Device Replacement"
- Enter the asset tag, description of the damage, and any supporting photos
- The request will be routed to your manager for approval

Step 4: Back up your data.
- If the device is still partially functional, back up important files to OneDrive or the company network drive
- Do not store critical files only on the local hard drive
- IT may be able to recover data from a non-bootable device upon request

Step 5: Return the old device.
- Once you receive the replacement, return the damaged device to IT
- IT will provide a return shipping label for remote employees
- All company devices must be returned even if non-functional for proper asset disposal
- Data on the old device will be securely wiped by IT

Expected Resolution Time: 5-7 business days after manager approval.
