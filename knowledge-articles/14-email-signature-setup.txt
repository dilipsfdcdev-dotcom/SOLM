Title: How to Set Up or Update Your Corporate Email Signature
Category: Software
Tags: email signature, Outlook, signature template, corporate branding, contact information

Problem:
You need to create or update your email signature in Outlook to match the corporate standard format.

Symptoms:
- No email signature is configured and outgoing emails appear unprofessional
- Email signature is outdated with old title, phone number, or department
- New employee needs to set up a corporate email signature for the first time
- Signature formatting looks different from the company template

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Open Outlook signature settings.
- In Outlook desktop: go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures
- In Outlook on the web: go to Settings (gear icon) > View all Outlook settings > Mail > Compose and reply
- You will see options to create or edit email signatures

Step 2: Navigate to the signatures section.
- Click "New" to create a new signature or select an existing one to edit
- Give your signature a descriptive name (e.g., "Corporate Standard")
- Set this signature as the default for new messages and replies/forwards

Step 3: Use the company template.
- Find the approved email signature template on the company intranet under "Brand Resources"
- Copy the template and paste it into the signature editor
- The template includes the standard layout, fonts, and company logo

Step 4: Add your personal details.
- Replace the placeholder text with your information:
  - Full name
  - Job title
  - Department
  - Phone number (direct line and mobile if applicable)
  - Email address
- Do not modify the template layout, colors, or logo
- Click "Save" when finished and send a test email to yourself to verify the formatting

Expected Resolution Time: 10 minutes for self-service setup.
