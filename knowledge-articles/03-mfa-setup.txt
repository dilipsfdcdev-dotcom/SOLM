Title: How to Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Category: Access/Permissions
Tags: MFA, multi-factor, authentication, authenticator, two-factor, 2FA

Problem:
You need to set up multi-factor authentication for your corporate account to comply with security requirements.

Symptoms:
- Prompt to enroll in MFA when logging into company systems
- "MFA registration required" message during login
- New employee onboarding requires MFA setup

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Download an authenticator app on your mobile device.
- Install Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator from the App Store or Google Play
- Ensure your phone has a screen lock enabled (PIN, fingerprint, or face recognition)
- Keep the app updated to the latest version

Step 2: Register your device with the corporate identity portal.
- Log in to the company identity portal (https://identity.company.com)
- Navigate to Security Settings > Multi-Factor Authentication
- Select "Add a device" or "Set up MFA"
- Choose "Authenticator app" as your MFA method

Step 3: Scan the QR code.
- The portal will display a QR code on screen
- Open your authenticator app and tap the "+" or "Add account" button
- Select "Scan QR code" and point your camera at the screen
- The account will be added to your authenticator app automatically

Step 4: Verify and save your backup codes.
- Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to confirm setup
- The portal will provide a set of one-time backup codes
- Save these codes in a secure location (printed or in a password manager)
- Each backup code can only be used once in case you lose access to your device

Expected Resolution Time: 15 minutes for self-service setup.
