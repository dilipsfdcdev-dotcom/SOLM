Title: How to Request a New Laptop
Category: Hardware
Tags: laptop, provisioning, new employee, replacement, hardware request

Problem:
You need to request a new laptop for a new employee or as a replacement for a current device.

Symptoms:
- New employee starting and needs a laptop before their first day
- Current laptop is end-of-life or no longer meets job requirements
- Laptop was lost, stolen, or damaged beyond repair

Steps to Resolve:

Step 1: Submit a laptop request via the IT Helpdesk portal.
- Log in to the IT Helpdesk portal (https://helpdesk.company.com)
- Select "Hardware Request" > "New Laptop"
- Fill in the required fields: employee name, department, start date, and business justification
- Specify any special requirements (e.g., high-performance for engineering, standard for office use)

Step 2: Obtain manager approval.
- The request will be routed to the employee's direct manager for approval
- The manager will receive an email notification to approve or deny the request
- Follow up with the manager if approval is not completed within 2 business days
- Budget center and cost allocation will be confirmed during the approval process

Step 3: Provisioning timeline.
- Standard provisioning takes 5-7 business days after manager approval
- The IT team will configure the laptop with required software and security settings
- You will receive an email notification when the laptop is ready for pickup or shipment
- Remote employees will receive the laptop via overnight courier

Step 4: Complete initial setup.
- Power on the laptop and connect to the corporate network
- Log in with your corporate credentials
- Complete Windows/Mac setup prompts and allow system updates to install
- Verify access to email, VPN, and required applications
- Report any issues to IT Helpdesk within the first 48 hours

Expected Resolution Time: 5-7 business days after manager approval.
